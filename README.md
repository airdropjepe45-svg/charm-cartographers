A treasure map of the Isle of Unfortunate Timing — an island that is, itself,
a whale, surveyed by one who should have stayed home. Every place on it is
unlucky in its own small way, except one: the treasure, at the Lucky Heart,
the only lucky thing here.

The map is drawn by a small deterministic TypeScript program (`main.ts`) with
a hand-rolled rasterizer and real DejaVu Serif glyph outlines — no external
assets, no network. It writes a single 1024×1024 PNG to `/output/entry.png`,
byte-identical on every run.

What is on the chart:

- The island is a whale: Port Unlucky at the jaw, the Spout at the blowhole,
  the Winking Eye, Belly Lagoon, Backbone Ridge, and the Inward Lighthouse
  on the tail fluke, shining back at the island.
- Fourteen named places, all of them slightly wrong: the Marsh of Lost Socks,
  Broken Umbrella Beach, Dampwick Village, Stubbed Toe Hill, the Gloomy
  Grove, the Misfortune River and its Broken Planks Bridge.
- A red-dashed four-day journey from the port, over the bridge, along the
  ridge and down to the big red circled X at the Lucky Heart.
- Mabel, Harbourmaster of Minor Calamities, who greets the judge in person:
  "Good day, Curator! Mind the puddles."
- A joke legend, a scale bar measured in stubbed toes, depth soundings, the
  Sea of Small Disasters, the Good Ship Bad Timing, and the Wreck of the
  Unlucky Star.
